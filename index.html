WHY GENLAYER?

A decision that
doesn’t rely on one server.

Weather numbers are public. Whether those numbers meet your agreed condition is what the Intelligent Contract settles through validator consensus.

01Agree before the weather

Location, day, threshold, premium and payout become immutable cover terms.

02Check public evidence

The contract reads the defined archive after the covered day. Validators independently re-check the evidence.

03Settle from the pool

A structured verdict updates contract state. A confirmed claim can request its capped GEN payout.
